## What will be collected?

- A recording of your voice speaking short Japanese–English robotic commands
- A transcript of each command
- Pseudonymous metadata such as a random speaker ID and broad recording condition

The public dataset will not intentionally include your name, email address, precise location, or signed consent document.

## How will the data be shared?

The recordings and transcripts may be distributed through a manually gated Hugging Face dataset to approved users for noncommercial speech-model evaluation and research.

The project intends to prohibit:

- model training or fine-tuning;
- voice cloning and speech synthesis;
- speaker identification or biometric profiling;
- commercial use;
- re-identification;
- redistribution of raw recordings; and
- surveillance or harmful uses.

No technical or contractual control can guarantee that every recipient will comply after receiving a copy.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a prompt or stop recording at any time before submission.

## Withdrawal

Before public release, a participant may request removal of their recordings using the private contact method maintained by the collector.

After release, the maintainer will remove identified clips from future versions and notify approved users when feasible, but cannot guarantee deletion from copies already downloaded.
